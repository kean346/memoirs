# Precious Memoirs concept scope

This is a demonstration for the business discussion described in the supplied WhatsApp reply: show how customers could choose and personalise gifts, inspect the result, and submit a clear enquiry. Bridal consultations are a secondary request type.

## What the concept demonstrates

- Choose a gift and see its generated product image.
- Change lettering, font, text colour, product colour, decorative motif and quantity in the live preview.
- Choose a gown and a separate style reference. The gown photograph stays accurate to the selected demonstration gown; the second image communicates the chosen colour/material mood rather than pretending that the gown was physically changed.
- Add dates, contact details and notes, review the brief, and save a sample request.
- Reopen requests in the demo manager; review personalisation, update status, create an illustrative quotation and export a PDF.
- Retain the draft and records after refresh in the same browser.

The homepage is intentionally shorter. The removed invitation, boutique chapter, process section and closing slogan are not part of the current presentation. The FAQ remains. Gift cards rotate as a slow decorative showcase without manual controls.

## Before taking real customer requests

The current browser-only storage does not deliver anything to the business. A real launch requires:

1. Business-approved products, available personalisation options, product photographs or approved generated imagery, pricing/quotation rules, logo and verified contact details.
2. Server-backed request storage with validation, secure private staff access, appropriate access controls and backups.
3. Tested delivery of enquiries to the business, through an agreed email or messaging workflow.
4. An approved privacy notice, retention/consent rules, business terms and fulfilment/consultation details.
5. Validation on the actual hosting address and representative customer phones, including media loading and the complete request handoff.

Those are launch requirements, not features represented as already connected. Payments are not required to demonstrate this enquiry concept.

## Media and styling references

The three category panels now use distinct generated bridal, gifting and getting-ready scenes, rendered into 12-second H.264 loops with gentle camera motion. These are AI-image-based motion clips, not newly filmed stock footage. The existing separate 4K hero footage remains unchanged. A generated six-panel material sheet supplies style references; it represents inspiration, not confirmed material stock.
