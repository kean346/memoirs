### Complete gift collection and media playback

`bridal-flute.webp`, `bridal-slippers.webp`, `bridal-gift-box.webp` and `bridal-hanger.webp` are newly AI-generated fictional product cutouts. No source resolution or visual quality was reduced.

### User-supplied interaction references

The bridal runway adapts the supplied **Maison Vell — Runway Depth Lookbook** reference: shared floating progress, tabulated depth, exponential drag easing, snap, wrapping look numbers and a synchronised product rail. It uses five generated gown cutouts rather than the reference's unrelated fashion sprite sheet. The gift cylinder adapts the supplied magnetic dwell, smoothstep cylinder positioning, perspective, pointer damping and layered faces to fictional bridal keepsakes; no bank-card details are used. Video categories adapt the supplied three-column vertical-heading design with existing credited local wedding footage rather than cosmetic stock footage. These interactions are implemented as scoped React components and CSS within the existing Next.js application, without additional animation or WebGL dependencies.

`public/images/runway-0.webp` through `runway-4.webp` are AI-generated transparent adaptations of the previously generated fictional gowns, used only as demonstration assets. They are not confirmed business inventory.

### Runway transparent adaptations

`public/images/runway-0.webp` through `runway-4.webp` were generated by adapting the existing fictional gown images into transparent product cutouts for the supplied depth-lineup interaction.
